Job Post Finder – Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026 · View online

Overview

Job Post Finder keeps everything it collects on your own computer. It never sends your data to the developer. Data leaves your browser only when you use two optional features: AI email drafting (sent to Groq) and email sending (sent through a mail relay you set up, to your own email provider).

The extension collects personal data, but the developer never receives it. The developer does not collect, receive, store or have access to any of your personal information, job posts, CV or browsing activity. There is no developer server, no user account, and no analytics or tracking. The extension does collect and store personal data on your computer, including the names and profile links of people who post jobs, email addresses, your CV, your email app password and the emails you send. All of it is listed below. It leaves your computer only through the optional features you choose to use, which send data directly to Groq or to your own email provider, never to the developer.

Job Post Finder is a Chrome extension for job seekers. It reads posts in your LinkedIn feed and search results while you browse, finds posts that look like job openings, and saves them to a table in your browser. It can also match posts to your skills, read skills from your CV, and help you write and send an application email.

This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it goes, and how you can delete it.

Where your data is stored

All data is stored only in your browser, in the extension's local storage on your computer. It is not synced to your Google account, and the developer runs no server that receives it.

  • Your email app password and Groq API key are encrypted before they are saved, using AES-GCM with a 256-bit key.
  • The encryption key is created on your computer and stored in the extension's own browser database. It is marked non-exportable, so it cannot be read out as bytes.
  • Everything else, including saved posts and your CV, is stored unencrypted in the extension's local storage. Other extensions and websites cannot read it.

The data stays until you delete it or uninstall the extension.

Data sent to third parties

Data leaves your browser in only two cases, and both happen only when you use them. Nothing is sent in the background.

Groq (AI email drafts)

When you open the email window for a job post and have saved a Groq API key, the extension sends a request to Groq, Inc. (api.groq.com) to draft the email. The request contains:

  • the job post text (up to 4,000 characters) and the poster's name
  • your CV text (up to 6,000 characters), if you have imported a CV
  • your name and email address from Email settings
  • your Groq API key, which Groq uses to identify your account

The request uses your own Groq account, and Groq's privacy policy and terms apply to it. If you don't save a Groq API key, nothing is sent to Groq and a basic template is used instead.

Your email provider (sending emails)

When you click Send, the extension sends the email to the mail relay address set in Email settings. The email includes the recipient, subject, message and, if you choose, your CV PDF. It also includes your email address and app password, so the relay can sign in to your email provider (for example Gmail) and send it from your account.

By default the relay address is a server running on your own computer (localhost), so this data does not leave your computer until it reaches your email provider. If you change the relay address to a server on the internet, your email, app password and attachments go to that server. Only use a relay you control and trust.

LinkedIn

The extension reads LinkedIn pages you already have open. It sends no data of its own to LinkedIn. While the scanner is running, it acts on the page for you, and LinkedIn sees this as normal activity from your account:

  • it scrolls the feed so more posts load
  • it clicks "see more" to read a post's full text
  • it opens a post's menu and uses "Copy link to post" to get the post's address
  • in search mode, it opens a LinkedIn post search for each of your skills in turn

When it uses "Copy link to post", the extension catches the link before it reaches the clipboard. It never reads your clipboard and never changes what is in it.

What we don't do

The developer never receives or sells your data. It is shared only with the services you choose to use: Groq and your email provider.

  • No selling or transferring of your data to anyone, including data brokers.
  • No advertising, and no use of your data for ads or credit decisions.
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or crash reporting.
  • No data used for any purpose other than the features described above.
  • No human at the developer's end reads your data.

The use of information received through this extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and control

You can see, export and delete your data at any time. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.

  • See your data: Open Table shows every saved post, and the popup and Email settings show your skills and settings.
  • Export: Export CSV downloads your saved posts.
  • Delete saved posts: Clear Data removes saved posts, scanned-post IDs and counters. It keeps your skills, CV, email settings and the record of addresses you have emailed.
  • Delete email settings: Empty the fields in Email settings and click Save. The password and API key are removed.
  • Delete everything: Remove the extension in chrome://extensions. Chrome deletes all of its stored data, including the encryption key.
  • Stop sending data to Groq: Remove your Groq API key from Email settings.

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request or delete on the developer's side.

Security, children, changes and contact

Security. Requests to Groq use HTTPS. Your password and API key are encrypted in storage. Encryption protects them when stored, but not from malware or from someone using your unlocked computer. Use an app password rather than your main email password, and revoke it if you stop using the extension.

Children. The extension is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13.
